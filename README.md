# The Price of Strategic One-Star Reviews

A report on strategic 1-star reviews on Too Good To Go, and what they actually cost independent businesses, and the wider food-waste effort.

This started as an argument about 1-star reviews left on Too Good To Go so that fewer people compete for the same Surprise Bags, and whether that was harmless or not.

I said it wasn't, but I didn't have the numbers, so I went and found some.

Turns out chains shrug it off and independent shops can't, and the blocked sale doesn't vanish, it turns into food in a bin.

Case studies are US, the cost and tax modelling is based on bakeries in France, the waste figures are global.
